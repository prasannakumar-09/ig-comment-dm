Set it up with AI

You copy one message, the AI does the rest and asks you for anything it needs.

  1. Install an AI app

    Pick one. Both work the same for this.

    Get Antigravity ↗ or Claude Code ↗

    If the AI later says Node.js or Git is missing, install them from nodejs.org and git-scm.com (just click Next → Next), then tell the AI “done”.

  2. Open an empty folder and paste this

    Make a new folder (e.g. insta-dm), open it in the AI app, start a new chat and paste:

    Set up this tool for me: https://github.com/prasannakumar0718/ig-comment-dm Clone it, read AGENTS.md and follow it step by step. Ask me for anything you need.

  3. Answer the AI's questions

    It installs everything itself and only stops for things only you can do:

    • 📲 Change 3 Instagram settings on your phone (it tells you exactly where)
    • 🔑 Make a free Meta app and click Generate token (it walks you through each screen)
    • ☁️ Log in to Cloudflare: a browser tab opens, you click Allow (free account)
    • 🔢 Tell it your Instagram account number (it shows you where to find it)

    🔒 Never paste your token in the chat. When the AI says so, you paste it into the terminal window, where it stays hidden. You'll also make up a password for your dashboard there.

  4. Add your first keyword

    The AI gives you your dashboard link. Bookmark it, open it, log in with your password, then:

    • New rule → keyword AUTO (or pick Any emoji)
    • Write the DM text with your link → Save rule

  5. Test it, then go live

    From a second Instagram account, comment your keyword on one of your posts. Within 5 minutes the dashboard shows it as Dry run (nothing sent yet). Tap Go live, comment again, and your second account gets the DM. 🎉

    That's it. It now checks your comments every 5 minutes, day and night, for free.

Good to know

How fast does the DM arrive?

Within 5 minutes of the comment. The tool checks every 5 minutes. That's the fastest the free setup allows.

Does it work on old comments?

Comments from the last 7 days get the DM (that's Instagram's limit). A new comment on an old reel works fine.

Will someone get the DM twice?

No. Every comment gets one DM, ever. If you test, use a new comment each time.

Someone says they got nothing

Ask them to check Requests in their DMs: messages from accounts they don't follow land there. Also turn off Hidden words → Hide comments in your Instagram settings. Instagram hides “spammy” comments (like many people writing the same word) from apps.

Is it really free and safe?

Yes. It runs on your own free Cloudflare account and uses Instagram's official API. You never share your Instagram password, and nobody else (not even whoever shared this guide) can see your account or your DMs.

I'm stuck

Tell the AI exactly what you see, for example paste the error message. It knows this tool's troubleshooting steps. Or follow the full manual guide on GitHub.
